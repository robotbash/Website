# Employee Guide

---

## Getting Started

Your admin will send you an invite email. Click the link in that email to set up your password. The link expires in 48 hours.

**Password requirements:** at least 10 characters, one letter, and one number.

Once your password is set, you can sign in at the app URL. Save it to your phone's home screen for quick access (tap the share button in your browser, then "Add to Home Screen").

---

## Clocking In and Out

The big button on your dashboard is for clocking in and out.

- Tap **Clock In** when your shift starts.
- Tap **Clock Out** when it ends.

A small vibration confirms your tap on phones that support it.

**Made a mistake?** After clocking in or out, a toast notification appears with an **Undo** button. You have 10 seconds to undo. After that, submit a correction request.

Your current status and how long you've been clocked in shows as a colored banner at the top of the dashboard. It also updates your browser tab title so you can see it while multitasking.

---

## Taking a Break

While clocked in, a **Start break** button appears. Tap it when you step away. Tap **End break** when you return.

Your break time is tracked separately from worked time. Whether breaks count toward your paid hours depends on your company's policy.

---

## Requesting PTO

1. Go to **PTO** in the menu.
2. Set the start and end date, and how many hours per day.
3. Add a note if you'd like.
4. Submit.

Your PTO balance is deducted immediately. There's no approval step for PTO. Contact your admin if you need a balance adjustment.

Your current balance is displayed prominently on your dashboard.

**Year-end notice:** In November and December, your dashboard shows how many hours you have left and what happens to unused hours at year end (based on your company's policy).

---

## Logging a Sick Day or Call-Off

1. Go to **Sick / Call-Off** in the menu.
2. Pick the date and whether it's a sick day or call-off.
3. Add a note if you'd like.
4. Submit.

These don't deduct from your PTO balance. They're tracked separately so your admin can see attendance patterns.

---

## Submitting a Missed Punch Correction

If you forgot to clock in or out:

1. Go to **Corrections** in the menu.
2. Enter the date, the time you should have clocked in, and optionally the time you should have clocked out.
3. Write a brief explanation of what happened.
4. Submit.

An admin reviews it and either approves or denies it. You get an in-app notification when a decision is made. If approved, your timesheet is updated automatically.

---

## Viewing Your Timesheet

Go to **Timesheet** in the menu to see your clock-in and clock-out times for the last 14 days. Entries that were corrected are labeled.

---

## Changing Your Password

Go to **Settings** (your name in the top-right corner). Under **Change Password**, enter and confirm your new password.

---

## Signing Out

Click your name in the top-right corner, then **Sign out**. To sign out of all devices at once, choose **Sign out everywhere**.
