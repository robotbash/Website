01 — The Property

Wild land, quiet roads, and cold, clear water.

Nestled in the quiet community of Hesperia, Michigan, Yoberosa sits on 300 acres of beautiful wooded land surrounded by the Manistee National Forest. Miles of Martin Creek run through the property — cold, clean water filled with Brook and Brown Trout.

The land also features its own beaver dam and a network of established trails for hiking, cross-country skiing, and snowmobiling. Seven dedicated hunting blinds are built throughout the property, positioned for small game and deer throughout the season.

02 — The Lodge

Built for gathering. Made for the outdoors.

Yoberosa sleeps 20 comfortably across six bedrooms and a loft with four sets of bunk beds. The Great Room anchors the lodge — a wet bar, pool table, stone fireplace, and a big-screen TV with surround sound.

Outside, a large deck overlooks the property and an outdoor hot tub that seats six keeps the cold nights comfortable. A full-service kitchen with restaurant-size refrigerator and double ovens, plus two full baths, rounds out everything you need.

05 — Location

Deep in the forest. Easy to find.

Directions to Yoberosa
From the West / Lakeshore

Take US-31 North (or South) to M-20. Head east approximately 17 miles into Hesperia. Continue through town on M-20 for 3 miles to Green Road. Turn left (north) on Green, travel 2 miles to Monroe Road — the first gravel road on the right after the White River bridge.

Final Approach

The Yoberosa driveway is on Green Street, 100 yards north of Monroe on the right. Look for the small Yoberosa street sign. Follow the two-track approximately ½ mile to the lodge.

From M-37

Head north on M-37 toward Hesperia, then follow local signs to Green Road.
